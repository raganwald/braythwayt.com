Callum:

Mr. Barge, exactly how long have you been coming here?

Mr. Barge:

Oh, a long time, young sir. Inordinately long, some might say.

Seigfried:

Mr Barge looked after me when I practised here alone, Calum, and he took care of my predecessor, I think, if my memory serves me.

Mr. Barge:

I ndeed. And the incumbent before that. (Siegfried) Oh, yes.

The days when the plough was drawn by living horsepower, and the motorcar, an innovation that many of us thought would not catch on. (chuckles all 'round)

Pity it ever did.

Callum:

You must have seen so many changes.

Mr. Barge:

Indeed, young sir, indeed. Some to our benefit, others less so. But that which matters most changes least, I find.

Mrs. Herriot:

What's that, Mr Barge?

Mr. Barge:

People, Mrs Herriot.
